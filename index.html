About Me

People on one screen, records on the other.

I'm a remote administrative and operations specialist based in Naga City, Philippines, working daily with UK clients since 2020. I work in three languages — English, Filipino, and Bikolano — which makes communicating precisely (and kindly) second nature.

My work lives at the intersection of people and records: one minute I'm on LiveChat walking a customer through their order, the next I'm proofing job documents before they hit production, designing the label layouts that ship with cut-to-size boards, or building the tracker that makes sure nothing slips through. Six years with one company has taught me what "reliable" actually means — showing up every day, catching the small errors before they become big ones, and keeping data clean enough that anyone can find what they need in seconds.

I'm currently completing a Bachelor of Library and Information Science — which is a fancy way of saying I study how information should be organized, protected, and found. The academic version of what I already do for a living.
